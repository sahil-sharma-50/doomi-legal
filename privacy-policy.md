# Doomi privacy policy

_Last updated: 2 November 2026_

Doomi is a virtual pet that reacts to how much time you spend in apps you choose. This policy explains what happens to your data. The short version: **your screen time stays on your phone.**

## What stays on your phone

- **Your app usage.** Doomi reads usage statistics through Android's Usage Access permission to count the minutes you spend in the apps you picked. These numbers, your daily limits, per-app caps, history and stats are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded, sold or shared.
- **Your pet.** Its name, outfits, colours, treats and streaks are stored only on your device.
- **App names.** Doomi looks up app names and icons on your device to show them to you. They never leave it.

There is no Doomi account, no sign-in and no cloud sync.

## What leaves your phone, and why

Doomi connects to the internet for exactly two things.

### 1. Purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat)

If you subscribe to Doomi Plus, Google Play handles the payment. Doomi uses [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives:

- a random, anonymous ID created on your device (not your name, email or Google account), and
- your Google Play purchase receipt.

Doomi sets no other attributes about you. Google's own handling of payments is covered by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### 2. Crash reports (Sentry), which you can turn off

When the app crashes, Doomi sends a crash report to [Sentry](https://sentry.io/privacy/) so we can fix it. A crash report contains:

- the error and the stack trace (which part of the app's code failed), and
- your phone's model and Android version, and the app version.

It contains **no** personal data: no usage data, no pet name, no app names, no screenshots, no IP-based user profile and no record of what you tapped. You can turn crash reports off at any time in **Settings → Privacy → Share crash reports**; it takes effect immediately.

## What Doomi does not do

- No ads and no advertising IDs.
- No analytics. We don't track how you use the app.
- No selling or sharing of data with anyone.
- No location, contacts, camera or microphone access.

## Permissions

- **Usage access** (`PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS`): to count minutes in the apps you chose. The data stays on your device.
- **Notifications**: for the few reminders you can switch off in Settings.
- **Internet / network state**: only for purchases and crash reports, as described above.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling Doomi deletes everything it stored on your device. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings does the same. To ask about crash reports or purchase records held by our providers, email us (below) and we'll help.

## Children

Doomi is meant for adults (18+) and isn't directed at children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and describe the change in the app's release notes.
